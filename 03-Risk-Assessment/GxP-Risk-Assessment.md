# GxP Risk Assessment

## Purpose
Identify and assess risks to product quality, data integrity, and patient safety arising from the system.

## Method
- Use risk matrix (Likelihood x Impact)
- Categorize risks as Low/Medium/High

## Risk Log (example)
- Risk ID: RSK-001
  - Description: [e.g., unauthorized access]
  - Cause: [e.g., weak authentication]
  - Likelihood: [Low/Medium/High]
  - Impact: [Low/Medium/High]
  - Mitigation: [controls]
  - Residual risk: [Low/Medium/High]

## Conclusion
- Overall risk acceptance and required actions
